About

Lina Orundas

New Media Design · Brand · UX/UI · Social · SEO · Photography

Positioned at the cross-section of brand identity, digital interfaces, and content, I create design solutions that strike a balance between high aesthetics and strategic function. Originally from Istanbul and currently based in Sweden, I bring a global, multi-city lens to my craft. My signature approach lies in blending the chaotic beauty and intricate depth of Istanbul with the sleek, quiet precision of Scandinavian minimalism, transforming rich cultural nuances into seamless digital experiences.

I don’t just design digital experiences; I give them momentum. From crafting custom identities and sleek interfaces to directing video and optimizing search visibility, I handle every layer of the creative process to launch work that gets seen and measured.

A creative driven by art, music, fashion, and visual storytelling, I apply a keen eye for detail and color to layout design and typography. Experienced in cross-functional group projects, volunteer marketing, and social media management, I am comfortable working within structured briefs and meeting deadlines.
