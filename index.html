I work where design meets story.

Rebrands, social campaigns, interfaces and written pieces all start the same way for me: a clear idea, then the typography to carry it. I'm a New Media Design student, originally from Istanbul and now based in Sweden, and that mix shows up in my work as bold type held inside calm, careful layouts.

( What I write )

  • Mood essays
  • Styling journals
  • Culture & aesthetics
  • Brand copywriting
  • Captions & social copy
Read Portal Press ↗

✏️ “A line from one of your pieces, or what you believe about writing.”

Every Portal Press piece begins with a mood: a song, a color, a street at a certain hour. From there I work backward to the sentences that can hold it. I draft quickly, edit slowly, and read every line out loud before it goes anywhere.

I treat the layout as part of the writing. Where a line breaks, how a headline sits against an image, how much white space a paragraph is allowed: these are editorial decisions, not decoration, and they are the same decisions I make as a designer.

Writing also pays off in the rest of my work. Brand voice, captions, campaign copy and interface text all get better when someone has thought about the rhythm of a sentence. That is the idea behind everything I put here: one voice, across print, screen and social.
