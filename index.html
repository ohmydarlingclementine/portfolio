About

Lina Orundas

New Media Design · Brand · UX/UI · Social · SEO · Photography

I'm a multidisciplinary designer who connects brand, interface and content, so the work looks right and also does its job. I'm in my final year of New Media Design in Sweden, originally from Istanbul.

My studies cover graphic design, web design, social media, marketing and video. I design the identity and the interface, then plan the content and the search visibility around it, so one person can carry a project from first idea to a live, measurable result.

Outside the screen I draw, paint, photograph, style and DJ. That habit of looking closely is what shapes my layouts, my typography and my taste. I've worked in group case studies, so I'm used to briefs, feedback and deadlines.
