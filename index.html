Hi, I'mLina Orundas

Multidisciplinary visual designer · Brand · UX/UI · Social · SEO

I'm a multidisciplinary visual designer based in Sweden, originally from Istanbul. I connect brand identity, digital interfaces and content, so the work looks right and performs.

I blend the depth of Istanbul with the quiet precision of Scandinavian minimalism, turning cultural nuance into clear, seamless digital experiences.

I don’t just design digital experiences; I give them momentum. From custom identities and sleek interfaces to directing video and optimizing search visibility, I handle every layer of the creative process, so work launches, gets seen and gets measured.

I bring a keen eye for layout, color and typography, plus experience in cross-functional group projects, volunteer marketing and social media management. I’m comfortable working to structured briefs and deadlines.
